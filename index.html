We Are Building a Sports Team

We're building a team that operates like elite athletes or artists. Intense, coordinated, playing to win.

The manifesto

“There's work and there's your life's work.

The kind of work that has your fingerprints all over it. The kind of work that you'd never compromise on. That you'd sacrifice a weekend for. You can do that kind of work at Apple. People don't come here to play it safe. They come here to swim in the deep end.

They want their work to add up to something.

Something big. Something that couldn't happen anywhere else.

Welcome to Apple.”

This was the letter handed to early Apple employees. We tried finding a better way to express our work culture. But the best we could do was copy-paste this.

You know the feeling. The sports team in school. The hackathon you won. Or an event where you made it work. It felt like play. Hard, exhausting, all-consuming, intense but never boring. Everyone was locked in. Everyone made the team better.

We are a small team, intend to remain lean and we play to win.

This will probably be the hardest you've ever worked. We don't romanticize that. It's just the cost of building something worth building. If what you want is a team where your work actually matters, keep reading.

What we value

  • Hunger

    You run toward hard problems. Nobody assigns you urgency.

  • High Agency

    highagency.com

  • Accountability

    You own outcomes, not tasks. No one here hides behind "I did my part."

  • Craftsmanship

    We ship fast but we don't ship sloppy. The details matter because the details are the product.

As Naval says you want to be around people with high intelligence, integrity and energy. The rest? Skills can be learnt. Or a skills.md can be made. One of the best products we will build is the team!
